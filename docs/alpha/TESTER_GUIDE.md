# Welcome to Ember's first closed alpha 🐉

Hi Andrew and Dimitri! You're the first two humans to meet Ember. Thank you.
This is an early test, so some things are unfinished, and that's exactly what
we want to learn about. Use Ember the way you naturally would, and tell us
what's confusing, broken, or lovely.

> Ember is for gentle habit-tracking and wellness. It isn't medical advice.

## What's in this alpha

- Ember's egg, hatching, and growing through the stages
- Daily quests and your own custom quests
- Check-ins (energy and mood) with optional private notes
- Focus sessions
- Bond and milestones
- The shop and wardrobe
- Settings: theme, save export/import and backups, privacy information

**Not in this alpha:** Ember's real artwork, opening the app with no internet
connection, cloud saves or syncing between devices, a "delete all my data"
button, the desktop companion, playdates, payments, AI chat or voice, and
app-store versions.

## Getting started

You'll get the alpha's web address from Andrew. The first time, you'll see a
Cloudflare sign-in page: enter your email, then type in the code that's emailed
to you. Only your two email addresses can get in.

### Add Ember to your Home Screen (recommended)

On iPhone this matters most: Safari can clear a website's data if you don't
visit for a while, but a Home Screen app is much less likely to lose it.

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address in **Safari** → tap **Share** → **Add to
  Home Screen** → **Add**. Then open Ember from the new icon.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the address in **Chrome** → tap **⋮** → **Add to
  Home screen** or **Install app**.

You may need to sign in once more inside the Home Screen app. On iPhone, the
Home Screen app keeps **its own separate save**, so pick one place (the Home
Screen app is best) and stick with it.

Ember needs an internet connection **to open**. Once it's open, it keeps working
if the connection drops.

## How your save works

- Your save lives **only on your phone, in that browser**. There are no
  accounts, no cloud saves, and no syncing between devices.
- The host (Cloudflare) runs the sign-in and sees your email and IP address.
  Ember itself never receives your email, and your check-ins never leave your
  phone.
- **Settings → This device** shows whether your browser has agreed to keep
  Ember's data. Tap **"Ask the browser to keep Ember's data"** once. It helps,
  but it's never a guarantee.
- When a save is replaced (import, restore, or reset), Ember keeps up to **3
  earlier saves** in the same browser as backups. **Clearing the browser's
  website data deletes them too.**

### Export your save (please do this often)

**Settings → Your save → Export save** downloads a file. That file is the only
copy that survives clearing browser data or switching phones. Export:

- before **resetting** Ember;
- before switching browsers, phones, or from Safari to the Home Screen app;
- every few days, just in case.

The file contains your check-ins and notes, so keep it somewhere private. To
bring it back: **Settings → Your save → Import save…**, choose the file, check
the comparison, and confirm. Ember always shows what would change first, and
keeps your current save as a backup.

## Reporting a bug

Send reports to **Andrew**, however you two usually message each other. There's
no public feedback address.

A helpful report has:

1. **What happened**, and what you expected instead.
2. **Steps** to make it happen again, if you can.
3. **The build version:** Settings → the line at the bottom, "Ember version
   abc1234". Or use **Settings → Privacy and feedback… → Copy diagnostics** and
   paste the result. That copies the app version, browser, screen size and
   similar details only.
4. **A screenshot**, if it helps (optional).

**Your check-ins and notes are private.** Ember never puts them in a report
automatically, and the diagnostics never include them. Before sending a
screenshot, check it doesn't show anything personal you'd rather keep. Don't
send your exported save file unless you've decided you're comfortable sharing
everything in it.

## Things we already know about

- Temporary pictures instead of Ember's real artwork.
- Needs a connection to open (no offline mode yet).
- No syncing: each browser and device has its own save.
- iPhone: Safari and the Home Screen app have separate saves.
- iPhone: browsers may clear website data after long breaks; the Home Screen
  app and regular exports protect you.
- No in-app "delete my data" button yet (see below).
- The first load downloads about 2 MB.

## Stopping, or removing Ember's data

You can stop testing any time, no explanation needed. Export your save first
if you might want it later.

To remove Ember's data from your phone:

- **iPhone:** delete the Home Screen icon (press and hold → Remove App), and in
  **Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data**, find the alpha's address and
  delete it.
- **Android (Chrome):** uninstall the Home Screen app if you installed it, then
  in Chrome open the alpha's address → tap the icon left of the address →
  **Site settings → Delete data** (or Clear & reset).

Menu names vary a little between phone and browser versions.
